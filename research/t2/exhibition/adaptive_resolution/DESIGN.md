Goal: a reproducible offline decision-support demonstrator, not a live signal controller. Scientific question: when does measuring more improve a constrained decision, and when is extra computation justified? Keep physical model, observation channel, belief estimator, planner, evaluator, interface independently replaceable.

No field calibration, no physical signal timings, no RF acquisition, no road actuation, K047 sealed, no Actions. 
R&D decision rule: prioritize useful end-to-end behavior over a new theorem count. Stop feature growth if cost, accuracy, reproducibility or interpretability degrades; preserve negative results.
