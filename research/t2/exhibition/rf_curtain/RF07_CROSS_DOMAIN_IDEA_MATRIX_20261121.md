# RF07 — Cross-domain idea harvest: low-cost intersection exhibits

Status: exploratory hypotheses, not completed prototypes. Cost tiers describe **relative required apparatus**; not verified purchase prices. Score before procurement against owned school equipment. Every demo is private/tabletop and labelled independently.

| Pairing | Exhibit mechanism | Strong falsifier | Practical tier | Role |

## Selection logic
A strong exhibition is not a collection of demos. Freeze one target: distinguish **empty** from **stationary occupancy**, then distinguish **pocket** from **downstream**, then test whether the distinction changes a hypothetical decision. Compare cheap direct measurements before choosing RF. Prior art is not a disqualifier; unsupported field and novelty claims are.
