## High-value critical harvest

- **Sensor placement (Hu & Fan 2024 + Zhang et al. 2026)**: classification-score maximization is not equivalent to physically observable *causal blockage identity*. Derive an exact finite-state equivalence class baseline BEFORE field classification.
- **Traffic Ear (2023)**: microphone and video camera were **both** in the authors' apparatus; prevent misleading 'camera-free' transfer.
- **Event camera (eTraM)**: motion events are informative about moving vehicles but can lose stationary state evidence; show with realistic stopped-object control rather than assuming 'no event = no car'.
- **Magnetic jam (Bao 2016)**: physically adjacent vehicles form merged sensor signatures (tailgating); do not equate point pulse count and queue length.
- **Fiber DAS (Thomas 2024; Corera 2023; Truong 2024)**: instrument and mounting geometry dominate cost/robustness, and the inferred signal depends on a moving vehicle track, not automatically a **why-did-green-discharge-stop** diagnosis.
- **Observability versus decision value**: even maximal Shannon information about state may not identify the particular state bit needed for a diagnostic decision. This mathematical counterexample is the next preregistered T2/RF method transfer.
