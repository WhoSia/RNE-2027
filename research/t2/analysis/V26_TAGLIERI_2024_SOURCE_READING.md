**Source-specific design:** Compare signals with permitted lefts (TW), signals prohibiting left turns (TWL), and roundabouts (RB) across idealized grid networks; use analytic MFD/NEF and AIMSUN simulation. Separate two-lane and four-lane road networks, grid sizes 10×10/16×16 and block lengths 125/250m. Metrics: free-flow speed, flow-moving capacity, trip-serving capacity, estimated fuel consumption.

**Result:** Single-lane RB networks compare favorably in the authors' single-lane configurations, while multilane TWL networks surpass two-lane roundabout networks in moving and trip-serving capacities. RB inner-lane gap acceptance and underuse are an explanatory mechanism. Fixed-control and homogeneous grid assumptions limit transfer. Distinguish network trip completion from intersection service and total traveled distance.

**Earlier Hallmark 2010 comparison:** Hallmark evaluated two real-world corridors with a local roundabout substitution and signals, while Taglieri analyzed network-wide uniform intersections. The findings are not direct contradictions because spatial scope, lane count, scenarios and metrics differ.
