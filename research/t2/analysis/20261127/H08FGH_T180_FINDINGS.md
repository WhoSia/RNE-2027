## T180 exact diagnostic decomposition
Let V_h(x) be fully observed, no acquisition-cost, finite-horizon optimum with V_0=0. Q_h(x,a)=g(x,a)+E[V_{h-1}(X')|x,a], D_h=Q_h−V_h>=0. For any causal history-based sensing/control policy pi in the same transition model,
J_pi=E[V_H(X_0)]+E_pi[sum_t (D_{H-t}(X_t,A_t)+c(M_t))],
t=0..H−1. Proof: conditional expectation and telescoping V terms, no need for belief stationarity. If observations don't alter physical transitions except through actions and cost only via c, QMDP's one-stage observation+action rule minimizes immediate conditional expected D+c (E V_h fixed). It ignores future D+c under the resulting posterior/physical state. This explains the possible H08H gap, not a sufficient causal attribution of all regret to a specific future stage. Standard performance-difference/telescoping identity, no general novelty claim. Next: distinguish actual future information loss using this decomposition and compare a limited-lookahead policy to QMDP, exact adaptive, uncertainty and budget-matched baselines. Tie cases are valuable falsification evidence.
