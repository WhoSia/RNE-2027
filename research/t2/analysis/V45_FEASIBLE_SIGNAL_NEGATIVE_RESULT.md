# T2 v4.5 — Feasible phase constraints defeat prior counterexample in tested grid (2026-11-02)

Executed locally with revised three-signal finite storage model: 48 world parameter sets (storage 2/4/8/16, main arrivals 1/2/3, side arrivals 0/1/2/3) × 3 splits = 144 runs; nine unit tests pass. New 8-step signal cycle: each approach at least 2 green ticks and 2 lost ticks per cycle; no zero side green. Uses snapshots so vehicle cannot advance across multiple successive links in same tick. Conservation of all arrivals and finite intermediate links checked each step.

Previous v4.4 witness exploited main-only split=5 with **zero side green**. With baseline split=3 and positive side service, and restricting witness comparisons to same storage and same main arrival, search yielded **zero** pairs with same aggregate baseline departures and disjoint sets of departure-maximizing policies. This is a grid-limited falsification of the *previous witness construction*, NOT a theorem about identifiability generally. The feasible-stage action set here is idealized: three signals share cycle, no independent offsets, not calibrated real signal plans, no legal turning movements or pedestrian calls, and no full vehicle OD or micro-speed trajectories.
