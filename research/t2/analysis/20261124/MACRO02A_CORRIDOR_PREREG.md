# MACRO-02A/03A — first offline corridor benchmark, prospective
Two serial abstract intersections: upstream shared FIFO Q with L pocket P; S crosses into finite connecting link R, L exits to separate destination (not R). Downstream crosses R to sink under its signal; old-stock eligibility and old-space rule; at most one upstream movement discharge per tick, left admission from old Q independent when head L, no double traversal. All external arrivals one/tick maximum, at tick end. Upstream and downstream each have 8-tick period. Upstream S duration g in {3,4,5}, remaining L; downstream service 4/8 fixed, offset o in {0,2,4,6}. These are abstract timing choices, not executable road timings. P in {1,2}, R capacity {2,4}. Cohort: all 256 eight-vehicle S/L tapes at two fixed irregular arrival clocks (A 0,1,3,5,8,9,12,15; B 0,2,4,7,9,11,13,16). Mask mod5=0 held out, others train, same grouping as P156 but different topology (not independent arrival tapes). 2048 worlds ×12 schedules=24576 arms. Horizon160, all clear; track stock conservation, capacity, per-tick transfer, integrated outstanding J, final stock, movement residence costs; sum S+L=J.
