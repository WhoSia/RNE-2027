# T162 / AGG02 — conditional coarse-graining and risk decomposition
Let W be the block-start observation and scheduled abstract conditions, U uniform on the eight tick positions, and X=D_U B_U with binary D,B. For a fixed W, expected block transfer m(W)=8E[X|W]. Independence closure h(W)=8E[D|W]E[B|W]. Thus m-h=8Cov(D,B|W). The covariance combines cross-world and within-block variation, not a causal coefficient. If Y=sum_{j=1}^8 D_j B_j, E[Y|W]=m(W). For squared prediction loss, conditioning and orthogonality give E[(Y-h)^2|W]-E[(Y-m)^2|W]=(m-h)^2=64Cov(D,B|W)^2. Population identity, not guaranteed finite-sample improvement. MAE is minimized by a conditional median rather than necessarily the mean. Finer nested information W' has population MSE improvement E[(E[Y|W']-E[Y|W])^2], by total variance, but learned finite-support cells and distribution shift may reverse this. AGG02 estimates these means on training seeds and predicts test labels from preblock state. It does not recursively advance a macro state, enforce forecast joint conservation across all movements, or establish policy benefit. To transfer to a conservative network model, require explicit sending/receiving bounds, node allocation and mass balance, and verify rolling prediction rather than resetting to realized microstate each block.
