# OPT01 — exact finite-horizon offline scheduling bound
Prospective. Six vehicles, all 64 S/L tapes, two fixed arrival clocks [0,1,3,5,8,10] and [0,2,4,7,9,13], P,R in {1,2}: 512 worlds. Exact FIFO/pocket/receiver old-space model, arrivals at end. Common first 8 ticks g4/o0. Post t8..95 choose g in {3,4,5} per 8-tick block, downstream offset o fixed for all post-decision blocks in {0,2,4,6}; no road actuation, no physical timing or switch loss model. All future arrivals known only to offline planning oracle. Exact forward DP merges identical (q ordered,p,r,Y,Z) states at block boundary, retaining minimum integrated all-outstanding vehicle-ticks and lexicographically first duration sequence on ties. For each offset compare three stationary g schedules and best variable schedule; final selection min cost with terminal stock=0 required. Reconstruct selected path and independently replay to verify costs and clearance; verify dominance over 12 fixed candidates. Report aggregate J, strict gains/ties, maximum gain, peak state width, expanded transition count, by arrival clock. Negative result permitted. No claim that this is a realizable controller; exact finite family benchmark, no prediction fitting or external calibration. Dechter variable elimination motivates state compression, not claim of reproducing bucket elimination.
