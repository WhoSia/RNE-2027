# T164 — separate forecast uncertainty from surrogate approximation
Let W denote decision-time information and A finite candidate set. Define population conditional risk R(a)=E[J(a,omega)|W]; scenario-library risk Rhat(a)=sum_j p_j J(a,omega_j); surrogate risk Rtilde(a)=sum_j p_j Jtilde(a,omega_j). Set f=Rhat-R, g=Rtilde-Rhat. If a_tilde minimizes Rtilde and a_star minimizes R, then R(a_tilde)-R(a_star)<=f(a_star)-f(a_tilde)+g(a_star)-g(a_tilde)<=osc(f)+osc(g), with osc(h)=max h-min h. Proof: Rtilde(a_tilde)<=Rtilde(a_star); substitute and rearrange. Uniform shared additive bias cancels; error interaction can cancel or compound. Empirical min-max regret over a finite scenario set does not control omitted scenarios without support or distribution assumptions. For AGG03B/C, true-world static oracle is clairvoyant and distinct from the conditional risk oracle, so reported realized regret does not estimate this upper bound. No novelty claim for elementary inequality. Next compare representation size/time under equivalent forecast inputs and test conditional scenario forecast generalization under changed arrival families, not more arbitrary thresholds.
