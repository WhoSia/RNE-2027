# P151 — movement FIFO, storage reuse and partial identification
2026-11-24. Prospective protocol, before enumeration. P150B scalar tandem proof does not cover competing movements. Independent small integer model, not T2 calibration.
Enumerate all 64 length-six S/L tapes, two arrival clocks (t=i or 2i), pocket capacity P=1,2, receiving capacity D=1,2, fixed tie precedence S or L. For each of 1024 scenario cells (64*2*2*2*2), use common predecision simulation t=0..7 with old-space convention, period 4 (S green first half, L second). At t=8 branch into 4 alternatives: period 4 or 8, each old-space or immediate reuse of downstream exits. Sink serves one vehicle at t mod 4<2, otherwise zero. All arrivals enter unbounded external/shared FIFO; no movement of newly arrived vehicle until next tick. Pocket admits only old shared-head L when OLD pocket capacity allows; S can move from old shared head and L from old pocket head, gated by respective green. Fixed precedence applies to simultaneous candidates if any; since exclusive phases, this is a negative-control factor and should have no effect. Do not infer sample independence from repeated tapes. Receiver exits FIFO; transfer cap one per tick; no vehicle moves two boundaries in one step. All arms drain by t=120 or explicitly fail, conservation and capacities per tick. Cost=sum outstanding after each tick. Report fixed-policy old vs reuse dominance or counterexamples, strict sign reversals (ties separate), and negative-control equality. 
Observation study: group worlds by decision-time observable state at t=8, treating unknown future tape and unknown event-order convention as latent, both policy costs computed offline (not a deployed predictor). Known P,D, timing family included in all sensor groupings. Nested features: (1) calendar/context only, (2) aggregate outstanding count, (3) shared count, pocket count, receiver count, (4) ordered S/L shared FIFO, pocket and receiver occupancy (movement labels of receiver unnecessary for sink). Report cells with conflicting strict optimal policies; sum and maximum of per-cell worst-case regret for pure decisions, and uniform-world mean regret under per-cell minimax rule (ties choose 4). Also repeat grouping including exact remaining exogenous arrivals and convention as oracle, clearly label. Group comparisons of sum per-cell worst regret not directly comparable when cell counts differ; compare maximum and world-weighted mean, and number of conflicting worlds. No tuning after observing outcomes. A null finding is acceptable. No road controls or physical measurements. Local JS execution in tool isolate is distinct from native Node verification.
