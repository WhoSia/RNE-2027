# P155B — independent movement resource dominance
Hand proof, not proof-assistant checked. Consider fixed exogenous arrivals, exogenous permission/sink schedule, old-stock eligibility, independent S and L external queues and independent receiver capacities D_m and sink service s_m. No cross-movement constraints or priority. Same admissible state at t=8, no state-dependent controls. Let A be cumulative arrivals before current tick. For direct movement let F,Z be cumulative receiver admissions and departures; Z'=min(F,Z+s). If permission on, F'_old=min(A,F+1,D+Z), F'_reuse=min(A,F+1,D+Z'); if off F'=F. For staged movement with pocket P and old eligibility, add E cumulative pocket admissions, E'=min(A,E+1,P+F); transfer uses F'=min(E,F+1,D+Z) or D+Z' when permitted, else F'=F. Each component is coordinatewise monotone in cumulative variables for fixed A,g,s, and Z'>=Z in feasible states. At the same state reuse weakly dominates old; by induction it dominates on every tick. Conservation and capacity hold by construction. Per movement outstanding stock is A_after-Z', so total J=sum over ticks and movements weakly decreases for any common horizon. This holds for arbitrary admissible common initial stocks, not just empty, if cumulative counts are consistent. It applies to independent direct or staged resources, not automatically to common external FIFO, common receiver, shared sink, state-dependent gating, or altered resource totals. It is a sufficient condition, not a necessary characterization. P155 ablations support a finite witness-specific mechanism; absence of increases outside original topology in those selected cohorts does not prove general necessity.
