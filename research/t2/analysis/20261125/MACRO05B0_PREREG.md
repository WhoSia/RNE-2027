# MACRO05B0 — spatial flux reconstruction under measurement uncertainty
Prospective 2026-11-25. Abstract periodic CTM, no real road or controller. Four unit cells, dt=dx=1, total density cap6, all 4^4 initial profiles in {0,2,4,6}^4. Two triangular FD families (v,w)=(1,.5),(.5,1), C=2; CFL satisfied. Truth F_i=min(v*k_i,C,w*(6-k_(i+1))), update k_i+=F_(i-1)-F_i simultaneously. Horizon 8 steps; verify conservation, finite storage. Estimate mean interface flux at t=0..8: exact local, homogeneous FD(mean density), exchangeable-histogram expected flux (mean over ordered distinct index pairs), observation-only noisy local/mean/hist, open-loop forecast from noisy t0 using same CTM; misspecified open-loop forecast from exact t0 using alternate FD, compare against truth. Noise channels: exact enumeration of all 16 independent +/-1 masks equally weighted with clipping to [0,6]; common-mode two masks all +/-1. For repeated observations, same mask used per trajectory for bookkeeping (persistent error, marginal at each t same as independent-in-time, not a temporal independence claim). For open-loop initialize once, propagate without further observation. Report sum errors, MAE/RMSE and signed bias by FD, channel, early t0..2/late t6..8, pooled. Include deterministic exact estimates separately; no sensor calibration, no policy optimization, no real deployment. 512 truth trajectories, 9216 noisy open-loop forecasts (512*18), 512 alternate-FD forecasts. No tuning. Exchangeable histogram estimator is exact conditional expectation under uniformly permuted cell order, not necessarily true conditional distribution of evolved profiles. Compare squared and absolute error, do not assume monotonic noise effects. Theoretical bound for equal-length ring mean flux: |Fbar(k)-Fbar(l)| <= (v+w)/N sum_i |k_i-l_i| by triangle and min Lipschitz. Check numerically all noisy snapshots, derive bound; no novelty claim.
