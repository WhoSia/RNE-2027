# I167B — matched-prefix counterfactual future uncertainty
Prospective after I167A. Abstract offline synthetic queue model, same 12 existing candidates and conservation kernel. Fresh seeds440..459, left counts6/12/18, P/R1/3. Construct three future variants from the same ordered 24-label tape: first four arrivals at ticks0,4,8,12; remaining 20 arrive at 16+4j, 16+j, or 32+j for j=0..19. Identical exact prefix/state and total label composition within each triple; only future arrival schedule varies. 240 matched groups, 720 worlds,8640 arm evaluations. At checkpoint no future variant is known. Evaluate each candidate on all three variants, per-variant static oracle, finite equal-weight group optimum, and group minimax regret (tie by mean loss then index). Report total regret, group count with no common optimum, max group minimum regret, per-variant costs, first witness abstract cost table, and gain of future-known oracle. Retrospective group optima allowed to know full variant distribution, not deployable predictions. Report whether prefix-identical futures can require distinct actions, not physical control or prediction of actual traffic. All conservation and clearance checks. No actual sensing or timing parameters for roads.
