# I167A — compact observation representations and support-risk calibration
Prospective after I166C. Pure abstract offline finite queue decision benchmark; no actual road or control. Same 12 existing abstract alternatives, same common checkpoint and conservation kernel as I166B. Train 48 seeds0..59 nonmultiples of5, known three arrival families (1728 worlds). Calibration seeds400..409, known + burst/irregular/blocks6/alternating (840 worlds). Final seeds420..439, same seven families plus new lateburst (first 8 arrivals spaced four ticks, remaining 16 consecutively from tick32), 1920 worlds. 4488 worlds x12=53856 simulations. All observations use prefix only. Compare geometry baseline; exact counts; exact history (counts+prefix total+left total+four arrival bins+four left bins); compact state (queue length binned 0/1..3/4..7/8+, pocket/receiver empty/partial/full); compact+prefix volume band (0..5/6..10/11+); compact+volume+left fraction band (<=1/3, <=2/3, >2/3, empty separate); compact+volume+fraction+recent trend (last8 count minus first8 count: negative/zero/positive). Compact representations nested among themselves but not exact history; do not claim monotonic floor across nonnested comparisons. Fit empirical mean and paired no-harm guard with thresholds support >=4,8,16 distinct training seeds, direct fallback geometry for unsupported keys (no hierarchical fallback, common comparison). Mean ties index; guard ties baseline then index. Calibration selection among candidates: maximize pooled benefit, each known/shift aggregate J and worst regret nonincrease and harm <=5% of all worlds; tie smaller feature index, guard before mean, lower support threshold. If none baseline. Freeze before final; evaluate all alternatives diagnostically; separately report new lateburst and prior alternating, selected pass/fail, conditional harm among changed decisions and overall harm, coverage and support, empirical floor/singletons per representation. No test tuning. No claims of real-world risk guarantees or sensor validation.
