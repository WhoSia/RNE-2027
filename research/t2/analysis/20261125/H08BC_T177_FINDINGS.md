## T177 restricted structural statement
FIXED admission maps (q,nS,nL), chosen action and arrival label to the next projected state and residual movement counts uniquely: service determined by q and action; each admission chooses first feasible label by fixed label priority independent of sequence; counts decrement by chosen label; append increments arrival count. Induction on remaining horizon via Bellman recursion (include cumulative residuals for fair objective) establishes count sufficiency for both defined objectives at every horizon for this exact rule. This is a standard Markov state aggregation argument, not novel general theorem. FIFO/SKIP need not meet transition closure. B and C do not isolate all model differences (actions, storage, timing differ), so not a controlled cross-family causal effect; within C, FIFO/SKIP/FIXED differ only admission rule.
