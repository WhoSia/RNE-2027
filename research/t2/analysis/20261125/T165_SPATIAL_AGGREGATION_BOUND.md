# T165 — spatial averaging and the finite-supply gap
For N cells on a periodic ring with equal lengths and triangular FD Q(k)=min(vk,C,w(K-k)), S(k)=min(vk,C), R(k)=min(C,w(K-k)). At an instant F_i=min(S(k_i),R(k_{i+1})). For each i, F_i<=v k_i, <=C, <=w(K-k_{i+1}). Sum over the cyclic index and divide by N: mean F<=min(v mean k,C,w(K-mean k))=Q(mean k). No stationarity assumption; this is a snapshot inequality, not a time-averaged network MFD calibration. Equal-density ring state attains equality; nonuniform states may attain equality too. For open interior edges j=1..N-1, replace mean k by separate origin and destination means in the two linear bounds. Full-network mean is not enough, since boundary cells receive different weights. The elementary bound follows from standard sending/receiving constraints; no novelty claim. Connects prior AGG01 temporal dependence to spatial heterogeneity, but two different aggregation problems.
