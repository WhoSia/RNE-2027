# MACRO05B1 — distinguish model error and measurement error through state reconstruction
Prospective after B0. Pure dimensionless periodic four-cell model, no real operation. Fresh initial profiles {1,3,5}^4=81, two non-complementary FD truths (v,w,C)=(.75,.5,1.8),(.5,.75,1.8); alternate assumed FD (1,.5,2). The two truth families are complementary to each other, alternate is not their complement or equal; explicitly report no independent physical evidence. 162 truth trajectories, horizon8, same simultaneous mass-conserving CTM. For each initial state and truth, eight noise replicate seeds0..7 (same noise tape across estimators), four channels: independent cell sign ±1 and common-mode sign ±1, each either persistent or regenerated at each t. Generate signs from high bit of LCG, seed=rep+1 (not independent physical observations; fixed pseudorandom finite cohort). Clip observed density to [0,6]. Compare measured local flux using correct FD vs alternate FD; initial-only propagation and blend (post-observation k_hat=0.5 model-predicted density+0.5 observed density, initialize observed) each using correct and alternate FD. Record pooled and channel-specific MAE, RMSE, signed bias, early/late and state mean mass error. Blending may change mass estimate, not a physical flow update; check each forecast step conserves its own mass and posterior remains bounded. 5184 paired noise trajectories, 46656 snapshot observations per method (six methods); no training or tuning, no claims sensor accuracy or control. Assess whether initial-only superiority survives FD misspecification and time-varying noise.
