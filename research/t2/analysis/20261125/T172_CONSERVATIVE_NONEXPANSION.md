# T172 — conditional stability of periodic conservative CTM reconstruction
For a ring of N equal cells and dimensionless step dx=dt=1, define F_i=min(v k_i,C,w(K-k_(i+1))) with K>0, 0<v,w<=1, C=Kvw/(v+w). The map T_i(k)=k_i+F_(i-1)-F_i is conservative. It is nondecreasing in neighboring coordinates. In its own coordinate, below critical density k*=C/v, receiving supply is C and incoming flux independent of k_i, while outgoing flux is nondecreasing with slope at most v. Thus the own-coordinate update is nondecreasing with slope at least 1-v. Above k*, sending demand is C and outgoing flux independent of k_i, while incoming flux is nonincreasing with slope magnitude at most w, giving slope at least 1-w. Continuity at k* establishes monotonicity. Constant zero and K profiles are fixed points, hence [0,K]^N is invariant. For N>=3, each neighbor is distinct (the statement here concerns the four-cell implementation).
Let m_i=min(k_i,l_i). Monotonicity gives T(m)_i<=min(T(k)_i,T(l)_i). Conservation then yields sum_i |T(k)_i-T(l)_i| <= sum_i (T(k)_i+T(l)_i-2T(m)_i) = sum_i |k_i-l_i|. Consequently same-model open-loop state error cannot increase in L1, but need not decay (different total mass alone provides a lower bound). For the mean flux functional Fbar, |min(a,b,c)-min(d,b,e)|<=|a-d|+|c-e| yields |Fbar(k)-Fbar(l)|<=(v+w)||k-l||_1/N. This controls flux prediction error in the same-model setting; it does not prove that a forecast beats measurements, that flux error is itself monotone, or that a different model has a shrinking error. For alternate map U, ||U(l)-T(k)||_1<=||l-k||_1+||U(l)-T(l)||_1; model discrepancy can accumulate. A convex blend with observations can change estimated mass, and is not a conservative physical transition. Standard monotone conservative map argument, not a novel general theorem. T172A checks 262144 ordered pairs and 40000 coordinatewise comparable pairs across 4 FDs, no violations within tolerance 1e-9. Reflection-complement k_i -> K-k_(-i) and swapping v,w maps the symmetric FD families to each other: these are not independent structure validations. The alternate FD in B1 is distinct from both truth families. No actual traffic or sensor validation.
