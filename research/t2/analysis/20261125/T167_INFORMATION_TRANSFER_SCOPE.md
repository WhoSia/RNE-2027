# T167 — population value of information and finite-sample transfer
For finite action set A, loss L(a,w), coarse observation Z=f(X), and fixed distribution P, any Z-measurable rule can be implemented using X by composition. Therefore inf over X-rules of E_P L <= inf over Z-rules of E_P L. This compares population optima under a common distribution and nested rule classes, not independently trained lookup estimators. Under shift Q, a frozen trained rule pi and baseline b have difference d(w)=L(pi(w),w)-L(b(w),w). If d bounded in [m,M], E_Q d <= E_P d + (M-m) TV(P,Q), with TV=sup measurable events |P(A)-Q(A)|. Derive by normalizing d to [0,1] and layer-cake integration. Training empirical mean cannot replace population E_P d without estimation control; empirical cell max constraint max_i regret(pi,i)<=max_i regret(b,i) is not a pointwise dominance claim, nor bound on unseen cells. New regimes can harm even with complete observations. I165A validates failure of prospective subset criterion, I165B demonstrates limited synthetic evidence for guarded mean rather than universal transfer. Both are offline finite candidate comparisons. No theorem novelty claim.
