## C. Corrections to the old 2026-11-10 ledger

- Han2024, Luo2023, Guo2026, Hidas2005, Tan2025/2026, Long2026 and Shafik2025: **held**. Do not list as missing based on older documents.
- `WiPCI` (open dataset / GitHub), IEEE 802.11bf-2025 (standard), ESP-IDF CSI SDK (vendor manual) are separate **datasets / standards / implementation specifications**. Do not inflate missing-paper counts by treating them as journal articles.
- Wi-Fi handset **probe requests** do not measure environmental CSI. Do not confuse MAC tracking with device-free RF inference.
- RF-CURTAIN currently has zero measured CSI trials, and `rf_pilot_evaluator.py` contains a test fixture with synthetic label-correlated features. Never claim physical detection accuracy from the fixture.
- Source provenance and true measurement validity are more important than originating the technology. We may implement prior peer-reviewed methods exactly, with explicit `REIMPLEMENTED_AND_TESTED` vs `SIMPLIFIED_ANALOGUE` vs `NOT_IMPLEMENTED` classification.
