# PHYS-07 — Observation latency and demand-regime falsification

2026-11-21 · T2 research · **SYNTHETIC_UNCALIBRATED**

## Decision and audit scope

Frozen k=5 nearest-neighbor phase selector learned only from the original 144
PHYS-04 cases, seeds 0–7, at demand 1800 vehicles/hour and left-turn share
0.5. No labels or future arrivals of evaluation seeds are used to choose an
action. Compare the same two future signal schedules at a tick-84 full-state
checkpoint with the established 240-tick vehicle-second plus 60×remaining
objective. All numeric claims below come from the *source-derived local
Python simulator*, **not** an exact-blob full GitHub checkout and not field
traffic measurements.

Two subexperiments:

- **Latency:** 432 original unseen-seed cases (seeds 8–31). Use actual
  snapshots at ticks 84,81,78,72,60 to simulate a 0/3/6/12/24-second-old
  observation while holding the underlying control/treatment outcomes fixed.
- **Demand regimes:** new seeds 32–39, 18 configurations per seed per regime
  (3 receiving capacities × 3 pocket lengths × 2 B-lane shares), 144 exact
  paired states each × 5 traffic regimes = **720 new policy pairs**.
  The trained policy is NOT adjusted or re-fitted for the changed demands.

These intervals describe only synthetic seed variability conditional on this
generator and fixed classifier. They do not cover calibration or model risk.

The low-demand/low-turn regime is a concrete failure of transferring a
signal allocation decision from training conditions. Geometry, counts and
positions are not sufficient to ensure safe or non-harmful action under
unmodeled regime shifts. Since all regimes are generated by the same
simplified engine, **this is not external physics validation**. Across
regimes, the predecision state and demand tape are different, unlike the
within-regime paired policy branches.

## Research verdict

Observation timestamp age is itself a decision-critical state variable.
Detector precision is not a replacement for information freshness.
More importantly, a frozen policy can fail systematically under turn-share
and demand shifts even if it performs well on unseen seeds of its training
traffic generator. Proceed to an explicitly prespecified age-aware
fallback on genuinely new seeds 40–55 (PHYS-08), then test that fallback
against shifted demand. Do not describe any age fallback as safety-certified.
K047 holdout stays sealed.
