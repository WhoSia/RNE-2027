P4 mesoscopic bridge has 1/3/5 junctions, 120 factor runs with side arrivals increasing in larger networks, and 36 controlled input runs; those cannot be pooled as a distance-matched congestion effect. Original P3 stopline and 2D swept collision remain unresolved.

Required P6 gates:
1. Time-coherent signal/turn/path clearance and upstream longitudinal viability: bounded braking BEFORE reaching a point of no safe stop; report infeasible states rather than rewriting speed or teleporting.
2. One-step numerical feasible-set integrator validated with leader future position, vehicle length/min-gap, and exact speed bounds, then extend to a multi-step reachable set.
3. Continuous trajectory swept footprints and moving pedestrian conflict by the SAME time interval, not only instantaneous polygons.
4. Cross-junction transfer keeps one ID, one location, full state (x,y,heading,v,vehicle class), link storage and no instant disappearance; validate network service bottlenecks vs actual measured discharge.
5. Unfinished vehicles and fixed-horizon occupancy exposure documented independently of completed journey times.
6. External SUMO matched case and at least one held-out observed intersection day before field predictive claims.
