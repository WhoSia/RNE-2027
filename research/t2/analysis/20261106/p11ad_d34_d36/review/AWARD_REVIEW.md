## Research and judging readiness

Strengths: detailed developmental chain, byte-sealed P10 original replay, rich falsification examples (time backdating, false HOL, false collision zone, finite storage length), reproducible scripts, actual distinction between simulation and external observation, new detector-error ranking reversal.

Crucial weaknesses: no successfully executed independent SUMO simulation, no site-specific field queues/phase data or validation, main microscopic engine stuck at 1s and no proven convergence, sensor noise model invented rather than fitted, time-dependent routing is known prior art, signal policy baseline small compared with existing actuated and adaptive controllers, large body of work makes an exhibition question less focused, student-direct work explanation must be demonstrable.

**Verdict**: A promising candidate for Gyeongnam regional and possible national advancement if the research is narrowed to a measurable phenomenon and empirically validated. Current progress does **not** substantiate a predicted prize/rank/award probability. Particularly a top national award is not supported by evidence today. The sharper exhibition question: 'Under measured downstream occupancy and queue spillback, when does detector error change the ranking of fixed-time and actuated signals?' Only after matched geometry, measured queue and sensor error distributions exist.

Do not publish or submit a substantially overlapping paper/competition work before verifying eligibility and originality conditions for relevant 2027 regional/national rules. Publication/precedent conflicts must be checked with official organizer.
