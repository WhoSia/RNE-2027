## Mathematical result
Let G be compatible states, partition by target signature. If one signature, W(G)=S(G)=0. Otherwise W(G)=min_c [1+max(W(G0),W(G1))]; S(G)=min_c [|G|+S(G0)+S(G1)] where both children nonempty. S/|G| is mean under uniform G. Induction on strictly decreasing |G| proves optimality. Optimize worst and mean separately; tie-breaking of worst-optimal tree does not establish secondary mean optimality.
