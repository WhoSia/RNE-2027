# T2 v6.1-P1 — Explicit Connector State (20261103)

STATUS: EXPERIMENTAL HOLD, not a promoted physically certified model.

Independent SINGLE-intersection microscopic testbed. Vehicles occupy exactly one of pending, incoming M/S, intersection connector C, downstream D or completed. After crossing the stop line a car stays in C until simulated connector travel and 4.5 m rear clearance complete, then enters D. No simultaneous downstream and connector membership. It does not yet replace the existing three-junction corridor.

Assumptions: synthetic IDM, dt=0.25s, accel 1.5 m/s2, braking threshold 3 m/s2, speed 11 m/s, 2m clearance, 60s cycle with M30/allred3/S24/allred3; synthetic quarter-circle right-turn radius 8/12/20m and lateral acceleration limit 1.8 m/s2. Only one exclusive connector; NO concurrent nonconflicting service, 2D swept rectangles, field calibration or SUMO independent verification.

23/23 tests passed (finite state, conservation, phase legality, follow spacing, rear-clearance ordering and monotone connector occupancy). 180 matched-demand radius experiments: 179 invariant-clean, 1 FAILED heldout run (main_heavy seed 203) with repeated stopline overshoot; invalid seed excluded from paired comparisons. 144 synthetic 2x2 path-length/speed-cap mechanism experiments: 143 valid; one failed (main_heavy seed 407, path radius20 and speed radius20), 286 warning timesteps. The artificial separation of path length and speed cap is not a real geometric intervention and must not be presented as a causal road design effect.

120 independent stress runs (3 load levels x 40 seeds) produced 8,910 vehicles; zero recorded duplicate membership, headway gaps, bounded braking warnings or reconstructed exposure errors within this finite grid. Those results do not establish general physical safety.

Review decision: state accounting improved, but vehicle approach-to-stopline anticipation fails in some out-of-sample cases. No full promotion. Next: continuous x/y/heading/speed plus vehicle body envelope, physically feasible braking planner, three-junction integration including finite turn pocket, heldout SUMO and actual traffic trajectories.
